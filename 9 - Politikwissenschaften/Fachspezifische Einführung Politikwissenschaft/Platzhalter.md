# Lecture Notes

The ngoization of feminism is a term coined in the 90s, describing a tranformation that happened to many feminist movements. A common feature of post-reunification germanies feminist movemnts was their lack of public presence and visbility of their activism. In the 90s many NGOs addressing gender issues and women's empowerment became much more visible than feminism as a social movement itself. Instead of small self-organized collectives, feminist organizations thus tended to institutionalize and fit into the framework of state structures and policies, instead of arguing for a drastic systemic change -> 'depoliticization' of feminism. 

This also led to a class and race division between NGO workers that where predominantly white and educated and women of other ethnic or social backgrounds that where more integrated in other political struggles. 

However, the term Ngoization also reduces the reality of feminist struggle to a binary opposition between these and past feminist movements, that are idealized as properly political.
