# Lecture Notes

Observations during conventions can be collected in the form of field notes. One problem with field notes is ensuring their completeness; this also includes behavior during a recording, context in which observations took place, etc. Therefore field researchers shouldn't strive for completeness but instead be wary of the fact, that their data might not be complete or correct.
