#### Thick and Thin descriptions
Wink vs. blink as an example for the difference between thick and thin descriptions. A simple action as a blink can convey complex meanings. Understanding this meaning requires interpretation from the observer. 
A thin description would only focus on the external, observable aspects of the blink. A thick description also tries to understand the intention behind the action by analyzing the its context.

#### Interpretative Methods
Have a large overlap with qualitative methods, but neither are all interpretative methods qualitative, neither are all qualitative methods interpretative.
Mudde and Kaltwasser on Populism is an example for qualitative research that is not interpretative, they make claims about populism that are shared between left and right populism based on qualitative research (party documents, manifestos, speeches) but are not regarding the reasons for why people are attracted to populist rhetoric, which would need interpretative approaches. 

Examples for interpretative methods:
- Interviews
- Participant Observation
- Discourse/narrative analysis
